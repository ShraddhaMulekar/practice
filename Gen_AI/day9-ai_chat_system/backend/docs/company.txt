==============================
AI CHAT SYSTEMS TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & COMPANY POLICY
==============================

1. WORKING HOURS POLICY
--------------------------------
Employees are expected to work 8 hours per day, Monday to Friday.
Standard working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM with a 1-hour lunch break.

Flexible working hours are allowed between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM start time,
depending on team approval.

Remote work is permitted for approved roles.

--------------------------------

2. LEAVE POLICY
--------------------------------
Employees are entitled to the following leaves per year:

- Paid Leave (PL): 20 days annually
- Sick Leave (SL): 10 days annually
- Casual Leave (CL): 7 days annually

Unused Paid Leaves can be carried forward up to 10 days.

Employees must apply for leave through the HR portal at least
2 days in advance unless it is an emergency.

--------------------------------

3. WORK FROM HOME POLICY
--------------------------------
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week
with manager approval.

During work-from-home days, employees must remain available
on company communication platforms between working hours.

--------------------------------

4. CODE OF CONDUCT
--------------------------------
Employees must maintain professional behavior at all times.

Harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate conduct
will result in disciplinary action.

Company assets must be used responsibly.

--------------------------------

5. PERFORMANCE REVIEW POLICY
--------------------------------
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year.

Evaluation criteria include:
- Technical skills
- Team collaboration
- Communication
- Project delivery

Outstanding performers may receive bonuses or promotions.

--------------------------------

6. DATA SECURITY POLICY
--------------------------------
Employees must not share confidential company information
outside the organization.

Passwords must not be shared with anyone.

All official work must be performed using company-approved systems.

--------------------------------

7. RESIGNATION POLICY
--------------------------------
Employees must provide a 30-day notice period before resignation.

The company may waive notice period based on circumstances.

All company assets must be returned before final settlement.

--------------------------------

8. HOLIDAY POLICY
--------------------------------
Employees receive 12 public holidays annually
as declared by HR.

Festival holidays may vary depending on region.

--------------------------------
