COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT

--------------------------------------------------
1. Working Hours
--------------------------------------------------
- Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Employees are expected to complete 8 working hours per day.
- Flexible login is allowed between 9:00 AM and 10:30 AM.
- Overtime work must be approved by the reporting manager.

--------------------------------------------------
2. Leave Policy
--------------------------------------------------
- Employees are entitled to 20 paid leaves per year.
- Paid leaves include casual leave, sick leave, and personal leave.
- Unused leaves can be carried forward up to 10 days to the next year.
- Leave requests must be applied through the HR portal.
- Emergency leave should be informed to the manager as early as possible.

--------------------------------------------------
3. Work From Home Policy
--------------------------------------------------
- Employees are allowed to work from home twice a week.
- Work from home requires prior approval from the team manager.
- Employees must remain available online during working hours.
- Internet connectivity and a proper working environment are required.

--------------------------------------------------
4. Code of Conduct
--------------------------------------------------
- Employees must maintain professional behavior at all times.
- Harassment, discrimination, or misconduct is strictly prohibited.
- Company assets must be used responsibly.
- Confidential company data must not be shared externally.

--------------------------------------------------
5. Attendance Policy
--------------------------------------------------
- Employees must mark attendance daily through the company system.
- Late login more than 3 times per month may require manager discussion.
- Continuous absence without notice for 3 days may lead to disciplinary action.

--------------------------------------------------
6. Performance Evaluation
--------------------------------------------------
- Performance reviews are conducted twice a year.
- Evaluations are based on productivity, teamwork, and innovation.
- Employees receive feedback and growth plans after evaluation.

--------------------------------------------------
7. IT and Security Policy
--------------------------------------------------
- Company laptops must be used only for professional purposes.
- Unauthorized software installation is not allowed.
- Passwords must not be shared with anyone.
- Employees must report security incidents immediately.

--------------------------------------------------
8. Communication Guidelines
--------------------------------------------------
- Official communication should happen via company email or approved tools.
- Respectful communication is mandatory across all teams.
- Meetings should be attended on time.

--------------------------------------------------
9. Compensation and Benefits
--------------------------------------------------
- Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.
- Health insurance benefits are provided to full-time employees.
- Employees are eligible for annual performance bonuses.

--------------------------------------------------
10. Termination Policy
--------------------------------------------------
- Employees must provide a 30-day notice period before resignation.
- Company reserves the right to terminate employment in case of policy violations.
- Exit formalities must be completed before final settlement.
